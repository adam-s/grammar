## What the lesson decides

**Nothing new.** `Adv` and `AdvP` both arrived at lesson 14.

## The finding

**The lesson is called "Adverbs and adverb phrases" and contains no adverb
phrase longer than one word.**

Measured: every `AdvP` in lesson 18 is one word wide, and **there is no
multi-word `AdvP` anywhere in the course.** _very slowly_, _quite calmly_,
_remarkably steadily_ — the shape `AdvP > Adv/premodifier` has no fixture and no
course sentence.

Lesson 17 spends all ten of its sentences on a degree word in front of an
adjective. Lesson 18, immediately after, could reuse that structure on an adverb
and never does. The two lessons are a matched pair that was not built.

The ten adverbs are _late, twice, immediately, again, yesterday, quickly,
patiently, afterwards, overnight, again_. Every one is clause-final; none is
sentence-initial, and none sits between the subject and the verb.

## The tests

**Omission.** These are optional, so all ten drop and leave a sentence. That is
the contrast lesson 14 needed and did not have.

**Movement.** An adverbial can usually front: _Yesterday those negotiations
resumed._ No sentence in the lesson uses this, and position is most of what
makes adverbials hard to find.

## Shortcut register

| Shortcut                     | What defeats it                 | In the course?                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| The adverb is the last word  | a fronted or medial adverb      | **no** — all ten are final       |
| An adverb phrase is one word | _very slowly_                   | **no** — course-wide             |
| Adverbs end in _-ly_         | _late, twice, again, yesterday_ | **yes** — only 3 of 10 are _-ly_ |

The last row is one the lesson does unusually well and is worth keeping.

## What this should change

1. **Build one multi-word adverb phrase.** It is a missing shape in both corpora
   and it is the obvious composition of lesson 17 with this one.
2. **Move an adverb.** Fronted and medial positions cost nothing and are where
   the difficulty is.
