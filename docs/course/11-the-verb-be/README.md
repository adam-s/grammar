# Lesson 11 — The verb _be_

Researched 28 August 2026. An author's dossier. See
[../01-introduction/README.md](../01-introduction/README.md).

## What the lesson decides

| Decision | In plain words              |
| -------- | --------------------------- |
| `vt:Vbe` | _be_ gets its own verb type |

## Why _be_ is separated at all

It links a subject to a complement, which is what lesson 10's verbs do, so the
separation needs a reason. There are three, and they are all formal:

- **It is the only verb that inflects for person and number in the past.**
  _was_ / _were_. No other English verb does.
- **It inverts on its own.** _Is she ready?_ needs no _do_. _\*Seems she ready?_
  does.
- **It takes a required adverbial**, which lesson 14 is entirely built on. _The
  keys are on the table_ has no counterpart with _seem_.

The third is why the split earns its place in this course specifically: lesson
14 could not exist without it.

## Shortcut register

| Shortcut                                | What defeats it               | In the course?                         |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| _be_ is the only verb with a complement | lesson 10, immediately before | **yes** — the two lessons are adjacent |
| The complement is a noun phrase         | _The answer was obvious_      | **yes** — 5 of 10 are adjectives       |
| _be_ is always a linking verb           | _The keys are on the table_   | **no** — lesson 14                     |

Four distinct asked-shapes, the most varied lesson in Stage 2 before lesson 15,
because it mixes adjective and noun-phrase complements.

That is the lesson's real strength and it is worth keeping. _He is a doctor_ and
_The answer was obvious_ sit side by side, and the learner has to notice that the
job is the same while the form is not. **This is `form:AdjP` versus `form:NP`
under one function, which is the whole doctrine of the app in one lesson.**

## What this should change

1. **One sentence with a linking verb that is not _be_.** _The hot soup tasted
   salty_ / _The hot soup was salty_, adjacent. Without a contrast the lesson
   only asks the learner to notice they are in lesson 11.
2. **Nothing else.** This is the best-built lesson in Stage 2.

## Rejected

- **Treating _be_ as merely one linking verb among many.** Defensible in a
  reference grammar. Not here, because this course's lesson 14 depends on the
  required adverbial, which only _be_ and a small handful of verbs allow.
