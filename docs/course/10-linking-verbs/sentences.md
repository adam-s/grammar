# Lesson 10 — Linking verbs · sentences

Drafted 28 August 2026. See [README.md](README.md).

**Status:** the table below is the live course text. Where this page measures a
corpus — shortcuts, counts, what was missing — it measures the one these
sentences replaced; the notes under the table describe what is built.

**The shortcut:** every one of the ten complements this replaced was an
adjective, and no verb in that lesson appeared in any other sense. English hands you the fix — _grow_,
_turn_ and _feel_ are each intransitive, transitive and linking, so one verb can
carry three frames in five words apiece.

The test is substitution with _be_: _The soup tasted salty_ → _The soup was
salty_ holds; _The farmer grew potatoes_ → _\*The farmer was potatoes_ does not.

## Sentences

| #   | Sentence                     | The step                                         |
| --- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | The soup tasted salty.       | the complement describes the subject             |
| 2   | That room seemed empty.      | a verb that is only ever linking                 |
| 3   | The sky grew dark.           | _grow_ as a linking verb                         |
| 4   | Our milk turned sour.        | _turn_ as a linking verb                         |
| 5   | The water felt cold.         | **the same verb, linking again**                 |
| 6   | The crowd remained calm.     | plain linking, a verb that is only ever linking  |
| 7   | That bread smelled fresh.    | a sense verb, which is where the confusion lives |
| 8   | A farmer grew some potatoes. | _grow_ taking an object                          |
| 9   | She felt the rough cloth.    | **_feel_ with an object** — the _be_ test fails  |
| 10  | Those children grew.         | _grow_ intransitive — three frames, one verb     |

## Notes

_The sky grew dark_, _A farmer grew some potatoes_ and _Those children grew_ are
one verb in three frames. _The water felt cold_ and _She felt the rough cloth_
are a second verb in two. That is five of the ten sentences doing work no
sentence in the corpus this replaced does, and none of them is longer than seven
tokens.

**No noun-phrase complement appears here**, and that is deliberate: _He is a
doctor_ is lesson 11's, and holding it back means lesson 11 has something to add
besides the word _be_. It does mean a learner leaving lesson 10 has seen the
subject complement only as an adjective, which lesson 11 must then correct.

The other rule worth an author's attention is that a linking verb takes an
adjective, not an adverb — _The soup smells good_, never _smells well_. It falls
straight out of the structure, and it is the point where this analysis starts
fixing writing rather than describing it.
