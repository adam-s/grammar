## What the lesson decides

| Decision  | In plain words                   |
| --------- | -------------------------------- |
| `vt:Vint` | this verb needs nothing after it |

The first verb type. From here the course's claim is that **the verb predicts
the shape of the clause**, and everything in Stage 2 depends on that being
testable rather than remembered.

## The tests

**Nothing can follow it.** _The baby slept_ is complete. _\*The baby slept the
blanket_ is not. The test is trying to add an object and finding the sentence
breaks.

**It will not passivise.** A transitive verb turns around — _the belt was
replaced_ — and an intransitive one cannot: _\*was slept_. This is the sharpest
test in the whole of Stage 2 and the course cannot use it until lesson 37.

**A verb is not intransitive; a verb is intransitive _here_.** _The tide turned_
is in this lesson. _The driver turned the wheel_ is transitive and _The milk
turned sour_ is linking, and all three are the same word. This is the single
most important idea in Stage 2 and it is the one the lesson does not show.

## Shortcut register

| Shortcut                         | What defeats it                      | In the course?            |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| The sentence ends after the verb | any other verb type                  | **no** — verb-final 10/10 |
| Two words means intransitive     | _She answered the question_          | no                        |
| A verb has one type              | _turn_, _grow_, _feel_ used two ways | **no**                    |

Two distinct asked-shapes across ten sentences. Every sentence is `(Det) N V.`
and the verb is the last word ten times out of ten.

_The tide turned_ is already here. _The milk turned sour_ is already in lesson 10. They are two lessons apart and never meet.

## What this should change

1. **Put one verb in two lessons.** _turn_ is already in 8 and 10 with no note
   connecting them. Making that connection explicit costs one sentence.
2. **The passive test should be named at lesson 37**, pointing back here. It is
   the definitive test for this lesson and it arrives twenty-nine lessons late.

## Rejected

- **"An intransitive verb does not take an object" as the whole lesson.** True
  and circular. The lesson needs a verb that could have taken one.
- **Listing intransitive verbs.** A verb is not on a list; it is intransitive in
  a clause.
