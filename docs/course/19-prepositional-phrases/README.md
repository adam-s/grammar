## What the lesson decides

**Nothing new.** `P`, `PP` and `complement` all arrived at lesson 14.

## What it is actually for

Depth. And unlike lessons 17 and 18, **this lesson delivers it.** Measured
nesting inside lesson 19 reaches two levels: _out of the barn_, _up through the
floorboards_. Five distinct asked-shapes, the joint-highest in Stage 3 outside
lesson 16.

This is the one lesson in the course where longer is the right answer, because
the content **is** length. It is 6.8 words, level with lessons 20 and 26 and
below lesson 21's 7.0.

## The tests

**The preposition takes a complement, and the complement is a noun phrase.**
_out of the barn_ — _the barn_ answers to _out of_. Substitution works on the
complement: _out of it_.

**The whole phrase moves as one.** _Out of the barn the cat bolted._ If part of
it can be left behind, the boundary was drawn in the wrong place.

## Shortcut register

| Shortcut                                              | What defeats it                                 | In the course?                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| A prepositional phrase is a preposition and two words | _out of the barn_, _up through the floorboards_ | **yes**                                |
| Every prepositional phrase is an adverbial            | a postmodifier — lesson 21                      | **no** in this lesson                  |
| The first word of the phrase is the preposition       | _out of_, _up through_                          | **yes** — two-word prepositions appear |

## What this should change

1. **This lesson should be the longest in Stage 3 and is not.** If depth is its
   content, it should out-measure lesson 21.
2. **Nesting inside a noun phrase is unambiguous; nesting after a verb is not.**
   _We found the key to the door of the shed_ stacks safely. _She read the report
   on the train_ is already two-ways ambiguous and the lesson does not mark it,
   which is lesson 27's job eight lessons later.
