# Lesson 40 — Final synthesis · sentences

Drafted 28 August 2026. See [README.md](README.md).

**Status:** the table below is the live course text. Where this page measures a
corpus — shortcuts, counts, what was missing — it measures the one these
sentences replaced; the notes under the table describe what is built.

**The lesson this replaced composed well** — seven distinct asked-shapes, second
only to lesson 15 — **and every one of its ten sentences opened the same way**: a
noun phrase with a relative clause modifying the subject. A learner could start
every sentence identically and only think from the main verb on.

**And it contained only nominal and relative clauses.** Measured: no passive, no
participial, no gerund, no infinitive, no coordination, no comparative. Six of the
seven things Stage 5 taught were absent from the lesson meant to gather
everything.

## Notes

Four of the ten open with a relative-modified subject, against ten of ten in the
corpus this replaced. A passive, a gerund, a supplement, a plain object and a
relative each begin a sentence here, so the first four words stop being
predictable.

The constructions now present: gerund, passive, participial, infinitive,
coordination, supplement, relative, nominal, two objects, adverbial clause. Only
the comparative is still missing, and it is the one Stage 4 construction that
does not combine easily with the rest at this length.

**_The plan drafted by the committee failed_ is a participial and not a
passive.** It means one — the committee did the drafting — but the model records
a passive as _be_ plus a participle, and a reduced participial has no _be_ to
hang the claim on. Lesson 35 makes that decision and this lesson keeps it. The
two stored passives in this lesson are _The harbour was dredged before the season
ended_ and _The station was restored, and the town rejoiced_.

**_The inspector photographed the engineer in the office_ carries two ordinary
readings.** On the event reading, the photographing happened in the office. On
the noun-phrase reading, the engineer was the one in the office. Lesson 27 was
the only lesson with an ambiguous sentence, and a final synthesis is the natural
second place for one.

**_The surveyor who returned promised to wait outside_ is the composition the
lesson closes on:** a relative clause inside the subject and an infinitive clause
after the verb, each with material of its own. It replaced _The guide asked the
visitors to wait outside_, which needed an analysis the model does not have —
[../difficulty.md](../difficulty.md).

The longest sentence here is twelve tokens. Lesson 39's are eleven with one shape
between them; these are eleven with ten. **Length is not the measure** — that is
[../difficulty.md](../difficulty.md)'s whole argument, and this lesson is where
it should be visible.
