# Lesson 40 — Final synthesis · sentences

Drafted 28 August 2026. See [README.md](README.md).

**The built lesson composes well** — seven distinct asked-shapes, second only to
lesson 15 — **and every one of its ten sentences opens the same way**: a noun
phrase with a relative clause modifying the subject. A learner can start every
sentence identically and only think from the main verb on.

**And it contains only nominal and relative clauses.** Measured: no passive, no
participial, no gerund, no infinitive, no coordination, no comparative. Six of the
seven things Stage 5 taught are absent from the lesson meant to gather everything.

## Sentences

| #   | Sentence                                                               | The step                                 |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1   | The inspector who arrived late reported that the wiring smoked.        | relative inside a nominal frame          |
| 2   | Reading the warning prevented a serious mistake.                       | **a gerund** opens the sentence          |
| 3   | The driver was given a map before the road closed.                     | **a passive** with an adverbial clause   |
| 4   | Fortunately, the visitors who missed their train found a room.         | **a supplement** opens it                |
| 5   | The station was restored by volunteers, and the town rejoiced.         | **passive and coordination**             |
| 6   | The clerk who resigned claimed that the deeds vanished.                | relative and nominal again               |
| 7   | The plan drafted by the committee was rejected.                        | **a participial** and a passive          |
| 8   | The guide asked the visitors to wait outside.                          | **an infinitive with its own subject** † |
| 9   | The tenant who complained twice sent her landlord a notice.            | relative with two objects                |
| 10  | The inspector discussed the report about the bridge with the engineer. | **two readings**                         |

## Notes

Three of the ten open with a relative-modified subject, against ten of ten built.
Items 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7 each begin differently, so the first four words stop being
predictable.

The constructions now present: gerund, passive, participial, infinitive,
coordination, supplement, relative, nominal, two objects, adverbial clause. Only
the comparative is still missing, and it is the one Stage 4 construction that
does not combine easily with the rest at this length.

**Item 10 carries two readings** — _about the bridge_ and _with the engineer_ can
each attach to the report or to the discussing. Lesson 27 is currently the only
lesson in the course with any ambiguous sentence, and a final synthesis is the
natural second place for one.

**Item 8 is daggered** for the same reason as lesson 34's items 4 and 5: no
infinitive clause in the course has its own overt subject, and whether the model
holds one is a question rather than a gap.

The longest sentence here is twelve tokens. Lesson 39's are eleven with one shape
between them; these are eleven with ten. **Length is not the measure** — that is
[../difficulty.md](../difficulty.md)'s whole argument, and this lesson is where
it should be visible.
