# Lesson 36 — Gerund clauses

Researched 28 August 2026. An author's dossier. See
[../01-introduction/README.md](../01-introduction/README.md).

## What the lesson decides

| Decision                 | In plain words                      |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| `fin:gerund-participial` | an _-ing_ clause doing a noun's job |

## The finding

**One distinct asked-shape across all ten sentences, and identical picks — 27 —
for every one.** This is the most uniform lesson in the course, tied with lesson 39.

_Renewing the lease took a month. Dredging the harbour cost a fortune. Auditing
the ledger revealed an error._ Gerund, object, verb, object. Ten times.

Nothing varies. Not the position, not the internal structure, not the frame.

Missing: gerund as object (_She enjoys reading_), gerund after a preposition
(_after reading the map_), gerund with its own subject (_The children's shouting
woke the baby_), and bare gerund with no object (_Waiting takes patience_).

Lesson 36 is also a length trough — 6.0 words against lesson 35's 7.4, and 27
picks against 35.2.

## The tests

**Substitution.** _Renewing the lease_ → _It took a month._ The clause fills a
noun phrase slot, which is what makes it a gerund rather than a participle.

**The _-ing_ word is not the main verb.** Lesson 3's tense test: _Renewing_ does
not shift; _took_ does.

**Gerund or participle?** Same suffix, different job. _Renewing the lease took a
month_ has _renewing_ as a subject. _The clerk was renewing the lease_ has it as
the main verb after an auxiliary. That contrast is lesson 24 against lesson 36,
and neither lesson mentions the other.

## Shortcut register

| Shortcut                            | What defeats it                           | In the course? |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| The _-ing_ word starts the sentence | a gerund as object or after a preposition | **no** — 10/10 |
| A gerund clause is the subject      | any other position                        | **no** — 10/10 |
| An _-ing_ word is a gerund          | _was renewing_ — lesson 24                | **no**         |

Three of three, and the lesson has exactly one shape.

## What this should change

1. **Move the gerund out of the subject slot.** Any of the three missing
   positions would double the lesson's variety, and _She enjoys reading old maps_
   needs no new grammar.
2. **One sentence contrasting with a progressive.** _The clerk was auditing the
   ledger_ against _Auditing the ledger revealed an error_ uses one word two ways
   and is the only real difficulty available here.

## Rejected

- **The gerund-versus-verbal-noun distinction.** Real in reference grammars,
  invisible in this model, and it would be a label reachable only by meaning.
